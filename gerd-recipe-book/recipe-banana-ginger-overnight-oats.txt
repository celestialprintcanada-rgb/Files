Banana-Ginger Overnight Oats (CelestialPrintCanada)

PRODUCE
Bananas, ripe (freeze 1 for the smoothie): 1
Fresh ginger: 1 small piece (about 2 in / 5 cm)

DAIRY & ALTERNATIVES
Low-fat milk (or unsweetened oat milk): 1 cup (250 ml)
Plain low-fat Greek yogurt: 1/2 cup (125 ml)

GRAINS, BREAD & PASTA
Rolled oats: 1 cup (250 ml)

PANTRY
Chia seeds: 1 tbsp

PANTRY & SPICE CHECK (YOU MAY ALREADY HAVE THESE)
Ground cinnamon: 1/2 tsp
Maple syrup: 1 tbsp

Not medical advice. Triggers vary by person. Check labels for onion and garlic.
