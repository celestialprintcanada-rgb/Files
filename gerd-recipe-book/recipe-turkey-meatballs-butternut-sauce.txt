Turkey Meatballs in Creamy Butternut Sauce (CelestialPrintCanada)

PRODUCE
Butternut squash: 1 small squash or 1 bag frozen cubes (you need about 3 cups)
Fresh parsley: 1 bunch

MEAT, FISH & EGGS
Eggs: 1
Lean ground turkey: 1 lb (455 g)

DAIRY & ALTERNATIVES
Low-fat milk (or unsweetened oat milk): 1/2 cup (125 ml)

GRAINS, BREAD & PASTA
Pasta (spaghetti or penne): 8 oz (225 g)
Rolled oats: 1/2 cup (125 ml)

PANTRY
Low-sodium chicken broth (no onion or garlic listed): 1 1/2 cups (375 ml)

PANTRY & SPICE CHECK (YOU MAY ALREADY HAVE THESE)
Dried oregano: 1 tsp
Dried sage: 1/2 tsp
Salt: 1 tsp

Not medical advice. Triggers vary by person. Check labels for onion and garlic.
