Carrot-Beet "Nomato" Sauce with Pasta (CelestialPrintCanada)

PRODUCE
Butternut squash: 1 small squash or 1 bag frozen cubes (you need about 1/2 cups)
Carrots: 4
Celery stalk: 1
Fresh basil: 1 bunch
Small beet: 1

GRAINS, BREAD & PASTA
Spaghetti: 12 oz (340 g)

PANTRY
Low-sodium vegetable broth (no onion or garlic listed): 1 1/2 cups (375 ml)

PANTRY & SPICE CHECK (YOU MAY ALREADY HAVE THESE)
Dried basil: 1 tsp
Dried oregano: 1 tsp
Olive oil: 1 tbsp
Salt: 1/2 tsp

Not medical advice. Triggers vary by person. Check labels for onion and garlic.
