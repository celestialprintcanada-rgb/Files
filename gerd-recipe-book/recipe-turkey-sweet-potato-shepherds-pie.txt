Turkey & Sweet Potato Shepherd's Pie (CelestialPrintCanada)

PRODUCE
Carrots: 2
Celery stalks: 2
Sweet potatoes: about 2 lb (900 g), roughly 3 medium

MEAT, FISH & EGGS
Lean ground turkey: 1 lb (455 g)

DAIRY & ALTERNATIVES
Low-fat milk (or unsweetened oat milk): 1/4 cup (50 ml)

FROZEN
Frozen peas: 1 cup (250 ml)

PANTRY
Low-sodium chicken broth (no onion or garlic listed): 1 cup (250 ml)

PANTRY & SPICE CHECK (YOU MAY ALREADY HAVE THESE)
All-purpose flour: 2 tbsp
Dried rosemary: 1/2 tsp
Dried thyme: 1 tsp
Olive oil: 1 tsp
Salt: 1 tsp

Not medical advice. Triggers vary by person. Check labels for onion and garlic.
