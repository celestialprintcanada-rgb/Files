Ginger Chicken & Broccoli Stir-Fry (CelestialPrintCanada)

PRODUCE
Broccoli florets: 2 heads (you need about 4 cups florets)
Carrots: 1
Chives or green onion (green tops only): 1 bunch
Fresh ginger: 1 small piece (about 2 in / 5 cm)

MEAT, FISH & EGGS
Boneless skinless chicken breasts (or tenders): 1 lb (455 g)

GRAINS, BREAD & PASTA
Rice (jasmine, long-grain or brown): 1 cup (250 ml)

PANTRY
Low-sodium chicken broth (no onion or garlic listed): 1/2 cup (125 ml)

PANTRY & SPICE CHECK (YOU MAY ALREADY HAVE THESE)
Canola or avocado oil: 1 tbsp
Cornstarch: 1 tbsp
Low-sodium soy sauce: 2 tbsp
Maple syrup: 1 tbsp

Not medical advice. Triggers vary by person. Check labels for onion and garlic.
